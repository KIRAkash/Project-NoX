Show customers who's handling their claim
The ask

Customers keep calling to ask where their claim is. Can they just see who's handling it?

Why now

41% of online claims get a “where is my claim?” call within five days of being reported. Each call costs £6.80 of contact-centre time, and customers rate the wait 3.4 out of 5. TWDIG/q3-review

What changes for customers

Once a handler picks up a claim, the claim page shows their first name, their team and the day they took it on. Before that, it says the claim is waiting for a handler and that this usually takes one working day. kb:customer-portal/claims

Who is affected
Customers tracking a claim on the web or in the app
Claims handlers, whose first name becomes visible to the customer
Contact-centre agents, who see the same view during a call
Nobody claiming by phone only: their journey doesn't change
Stakeholders
Claims operations: owns the handler roster and the wording
Data protection: approves showing first names only
Contact centre: briefs agents before launch
Done when
Customers can see who is handling their claim without calling
The page says clearly when nobody is assigned yet
Agents and customers see the same thing
“Where is my claim?” calls fall by half within a quarter
Out of scope
Messaging the handler from the claim page
Showing a handler's phone number or email
Open questions
Should the customer be told when their handler changes?
Do motor claims need the same view at launch?
Signed · Claims ops
Product owner
02-product.md
status: approved · seat: product owner · builds on: 01-business.md · version 4
Product spec
Goal

Customers know who owns their claim at every stage, so they stop calling to find out.

User stories
As a customer, I can see who is handling my claim and since when.
As a customer, I know when nobody has picked up my claim yet.
As a contact-centre agent, I see exactly what the customer sees.
Acceptance criteria
AC-1 An assigned claim shows the handler's first name and team
AC-2 An unassigned claim shows “Waiting for a handler”
AC-3 A reassigned claim shows the new handler within a minute kb:claims-management/assignment
AC-4 No surname, phone number or email is ever shown
AC-5 The contact-centre tool shows the same card
AC-6 The card works with screen readers and at phone width
Edge cases
Claims held by a team queue, not a person
Handlers on leave whose claims move for a while
Claims closed before anyone was assigned
Copy

“Sam from the Home Claims team is handling your claim. They picked it up on 12 October.”

“Your claim is waiting for a handler. This usually takes one working day.”

Analytics
claim.handler.viewed with claim state and channel
Success metric

“Where is my claim?” calls per 100 online claims: from 41 to under 20 within a quarter. TWDIG/q3-review

Rollout
Home claims first for two weeks, then motor
Non-goals
Live chat with handlers

Engineering lead
03-engineering.md
status: approved · seat: engineering lead · builds on: 02-product.md · version 2
Engineering design
Scope

Two applications change: claims-management and customer-portal. Nothing else. kb:claims-management/overview

Applications changing
claims-management (Claims · Handling squad): returns the current handler
customer-portal (Digital Channels): shows it on the claim page
Contracts

GET /v1/claims/{id} gains an optional handler field. Additive: no existing field changes. kb:claims-management/contracts

handler: {
  firstName: string
  team: string
  assignedAt: date-time
} | null

claims.handler.assigned is already published and has no consumer yet. Reuse it; no new event. kb:claims-management/adr-0002

Must not change
POST /v1/claims in claims-intake and claims.claim.reported
Calls to fraud-scoring and payments-gateway
Data and privacy
First name and team only, read from the handler roster
No staff identifiers in the portal's logs
Failure modes
Roster slow: return the claim without handler; the portal shows the waiting state
Event lag: alert when a reassignment takes over 5 minutes
Risks
Team queues with no single owner: show the team name instead
Rollback

Turn the claims.show-handler flag off. The field stays and is ignored.

Decision

ADR-0002 keeps assignment in claims-management; the portal only reads it.
